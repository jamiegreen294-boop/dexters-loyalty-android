Dexter’s promotion

Spin to Win Terms

Promoter

Promoter: Dexters, 10A Dundasvale Court, Glasgow, G4 0JS, Scotland. The customer-facing brand may be displayed as Dexter’s. Contact hello@dextersspot.co.uk or 0141 473 5249.

Eligibility and entry

Spin to Win is available to eligible signed-in Dexter’s Loyalty App customer accounts. Unless the app clearly states otherwise, each eligible customer account receives a maximum of one promotional spin per day. Staff/admin/test accounts and fraudulent or duplicate customer accounts are excluded.

No separate entry fee

No separate payment is charged to use an available Spin to Win entry. Any future promotion requiring a purchase, payment or alternative entry route must have separate terms reviewed before launch.

Prize schedule

The current configured prize schedule is:

  • one £2 discount on an eligible order over £10, assigned once at a randomly selected spin number between spins 50 and 250;
  • one free coffee and cake, assigned once at a randomly selected spin number between spins 250 and 500;
  • one free toastie, assigned once at a randomly selected spin number between spins 500 and 700;
  • one £2 discount on an eligible order over £10, assigned once at a randomly selected spin number between spins 700 and 999; and
  • at spin number 1000, one randomly selected eligible meal.

This cycle and any reset mechanic must operate exactly as configured and must not be represented in a misleading way. If the prize mechanic changes, these terms and the significant conditions shown in the app must be updated before the changed promotion is used.

Prize redemption

All Spin to Win prizes must be claimed and redeemed through the signed-in Dexter’s Loyalty App as part of placing a Dexter’s collection order. Prizes cannot be redeemed directly in store, at the counter, by showing a QR code to staff, through the Foodhub staff terminal, or through any other staff device.

Winning rewards are personal to the winning account, are non-transferable, may be used once only and have no cash alternative unless expressly stated. A Spin to Win prize expires 30 days after the date it is won, and the app must show the customer a clear use-by date.

Starting a claim does not by itself use the prize. The prize is only marked used when the related collection order is successfully submitted. If the customer leaves the order before submission, the prize remains available until it is used or reaches its expiry date.

Availability and equivalents

Where a specific food prize is temporarily unavailable, Dexter’s may offer a reasonable equivalent of similar nature and value. A substitute should not materially reduce the advertised value of the prize without the customer’s agreement.

Fair administration

Dexter’s must administer the promotion fairly, keep the configured prize mechanism consistent during a live cycle, and must not retrospectively alter a valid result simply to avoid awarding a prize. Genuine entries should not be disadvantaged by later changes except where an exceptional change is legally justified and clearly communicated.

Misuse and technical faults

Dexter’s may reject or reverse entries or rewards resulting from fraud, tampering, duplicate accounts, automated abuse, manipulation or an obvious technical error. Technical faults should be investigated fairly; a fault does not by itself create a right to a prize not validly awarded.

Changes or ending the promotion

Dexter’s may end Spin to Win for future entries or replace it with another promotion. Material changes affecting eligibility, prize availability, entry limits or mechanics should be communicated clearly before they apply. Existing valid prizes should be honoured in accordance with their displayed conditions unless the law permits otherwise.

Consumer rights

Nothing in these terms affects statutory consumer rights relating to purchases made separately from the free promotional entry.
