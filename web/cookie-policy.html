Cookie & Local Storage Policy

What the app uses

The Dexter’s website and Loyalty App may use cookies, browser local storage, session storage and similar technologies to keep customers signed in, protect accounts, remember essential preferences, prevent duplicate rewards and operate core app functions.

Strictly necessary technologies

Technologies that are strictly necessary to provide a service requested by the customer, maintain security or complete core app functions may be used without optional marketing consent where permitted by UK law.

Non-essential technologies

Analytics, advertising, profiling or similar non-essential technologies must not be set or accessed until any consent required by UK privacy and electronic communications law has been obtained. Consent must be freely given, specific and informed, and customers must be able to withdraw it as easily as they gave it.

Third parties

Service providers may use technologies necessary to provide authentication, hosting, payment or communications services. Dexter’s should keep an up-to-date record of the technologies actually used, their purposes and retention periods, and update this policy if new non-essential services are introduced.

Your choices

Where optional technologies are used, the app should provide a clear way to accept or reject them before activation and a way to revisit those choices later. Blocking strictly necessary storage in browser settings may prevent sign-in or other core functions from working correctly.

Last reviewed: 3 September 2026. This policy must reflect the technologies actually deployed in the live service.
