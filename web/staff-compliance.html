Staff guidance

Legal & Compliance Checklist

Operational guidance for Dexter’s staff. Follow this alongside current recipes, allergen records, staff procedures and the customer-facing legal policies.

Allergens & food safety

  • Never guess whether an allergen is present.
  • Check the current written recipe, supplier label and allergen record, including sauces, sides, toppings and substitutions.
  • Allergen information must be available before an online order is completed and again when the food is supplied.
  • For serious allergies or coeliac requirements, check preparation and cross-contact risks before accepting the order.
  • If information cannot be verified, tell the customer rather than promising the food is safe.

Open customer Allergen Information

Customer orders & consumer rights

  • Make sure prices, compulsory charges, selected extras and collection details are clear before an order is submitted.
  • Do not add paid extras without the customer's active choice.
  • If Dexter’s cannot fulfil a paid order, provide the refund or other remedy legally due.
  • Do not refuse a valid statutory remedy because of wording in a promotion, deposit or refund policy.
  • Handle complaints fairly and keep useful order records where appropriate.

Terms & Conditions · Complaints Policy

UK GDPR & customer data

  • Only access customer information when needed for your role.
  • Do not share staff logins or customer information unnecessarily.
  • Never ask customers to send passwords or full payment-card details.
  • Use customer information only for the purposes Dexter’s has told customers about.
  • Pass privacy-rights requests, suspected account compromises or suspected data breaches to management promptly.
  • Remove or change staff access when roles change or employment ends.

Privacy & Data Protection · Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Promotions, points & rewards

  • Run Spin to Win and receipt promotions exactly as advertised.
  • Do not alter a genuine winning result to avoid awarding a prize.
  • Apply minimum spend, expiry and eligible-item restrictions exactly as displayed to the customer.
  • Prevent duplicate QR redemption and duplicate point awards.
  • Only reverse rewards for a genuine error, fraud, duplicate use, tampering or abuse.

Spin to Win Terms · Receipt Reward Terms

Sunday Roast pre-orders

  • The £4.99 deposit is part-payment, not an extra charge.
  • Follow the published cancellation and refund rules consistently.
  • If Dexter’s cancels or cannot supply the order, refund the amount legally due unless the customer agrees to an alternative.
  • Do not describe every deposit as automatically non-refundable.

Sunday Roast Deposit & Refund Policy

Security

  • Use individual staff accounts where provided and keep credentials private.
  • Do not leave staff/admin screens signed in on unattended devices.
  • Report suspicious logins, unusual QR activity, unauthorised access or possible data loss immediately.
  • Do not bypass security controls to make a customer transaction work.

When the app or business changes

Before launching a new payment method, marketing tracker, promotion, customer-data use, ordering method or other major feature, check whether the customer legal information needs updating first.
