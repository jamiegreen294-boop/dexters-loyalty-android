Food safety

Allergen Information

Please check allergen information before placing an order, especially if you have a food allergy, intolerance or coeliac disease.

Important — check before you order

Recipes, suppliers and substitutions can change. Dexter’s staff must never guess about allergens. If the item-specific information you need is not displayed in the ordering screen, contact Dexter’s before completing your order so the current written ingredient/allergen information can be checked.

Item-specific allergen information

Allergen information for the exact food, sauce, drink, side or modifier you are considering must be available before the purchase is completed. Where it is not shown directly online, ask Dexter’s before ordering and we will check the current written allergen record, recipe and relevant product information without charge.

Tell staff about serious allergies when collecting as well, so the kitchen can check the current preparation and cross-contact risk for the order.

Cross-contact

Dexter’s prepares a wide range of foods in the same working kitchen. Cross-contact may be possible depending on the ingredients, equipment and preparation method. Staff should check the current preparation process for the specific item rather than making a general “allergen free” promise.

Online and collection orders

For distance orders, allergen information must be available before the purchase is completed and again when the food is supplied where required. Dexter’s keeps written allergen information alongside current recipes and supplier/product information and makes it available to customers without charge.

Staff procedure

  • Never guess whether an allergen is present.
  • Check the current recipe/product label and written allergen record.
  • Check sauces, toppings, sides, modifiers and substitutions as well as the main item.
  • Explain known cross-contact risks honestly.
  • If the information cannot be confirmed, tell the customer that before the order is completed rather than giving an unsafe answer.
Last reviewed: 3 September 2026. This information is designed for Dexter’s operations in Scotland and must be maintained alongside the kitchen’s current written allergen records, recipes and supplier labels.
