Food safety

Allergen Information

Please check allergen information before placing an order, especially if you have a food allergy, intolerance or coeliac disease.

Important — check before you order

Recipes, suppliers, brands and substitutions can change. Dexter’s staff must never guess about allergens. If the item-specific information you need is not displayed in the ordering screen, contact Dexter’s before completing your order so the current written recipe, ingredient and allergen information can be checked.

Item-specific information

Allergen information for the exact food, sauce, drink, side, topping, modifier or substitution being considered must be available before the purchase is concluded. Where it is not displayed directly online, ask Dexter’s before ordering and staff will check the current written allergen record, recipe and relevant product information without charge.

Distance and collection orders

For orders made through the website, Loyalty App, phone or other distance method, mandatory allergen information must be available to the customer before the purchase is concluded and again when the food is supplied. Dexter’s should provide the information in writing with the food or make it available verbally at supply in accordance with the applicable rules. The information must be current, accurate and provided without an additional charge.

Cross-contact

Dexter’s prepares a wide range of foods in a shared working kitchen. Cross-contact may be possible depending on ingredients, equipment, fryers, utensils, storage and preparation methods. Staff should check the current process for the specific order rather than making a general “allergen free” or “safe” promise that cannot be verified.

Serious allergies and coeliac disease

Tell Dexter’s about a serious allergy or coeliac requirement before placing the order and remind staff when collecting. This allows the kitchen to check the current recipe, product labels, preparation method and known cross-contact risks. If Dexter’s cannot verify the information or manage the risk safely, staff must say so rather than guess.

Staff procedure

  • Never guess whether an allergen is present.
  • Use the current written recipe, product label and allergen record.
  • Check sauces, toppings, sides, drinks, modifiers and substitutions as well as the main item.
  • Check shared fryers, utensils, surfaces and other known cross-contact routes where relevant.
  • If information cannot be confirmed, tell the customer before the order is concluded.
  • If an ingredient or supplier changes, update the written allergen information before relying on it.
Last reviewed: 3 September 2026. This information is designed for Dexter’s operations in Scotland and must be maintained alongside current kitchen allergen records, recipes and supplier labels.
