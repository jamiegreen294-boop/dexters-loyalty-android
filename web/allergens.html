Food safety

Allergen Information

Please check allergen information before placing an order, especially if you have a food allergy, intolerance or coeliac disease.

Important

Recipes, suppliers and substitutions can change. Dexter’s staff must never guess about allergens. If you need allergen information about a specific item, please ask before completing your order so the current ingredient and preparation information can be checked.

How to get item-specific information

Before placing an online order, customers can contact Dexter’s at hello@dextersspot.co.uk or use the normal business contact route and ask for the current allergen information for the exact dish, sauce, drink or modifier they are considering.

Where item-specific allergen information is displayed in the website or Loyalty App, customers should still tell staff about serious allergies so the kitchen can consider cross-contact risks.

Cross-contact

Dexter’s prepares a wide range of foods in the same kitchen. Even where an allergen is not an intentional ingredient, cross-contact may be possible depending on the item, equipment and preparation method. Customers with severe allergies should speak to staff before ordering so the current preparation process can be checked.

Online and collection orders

Allergen information must be available before the purchase is completed and again when the food is supplied where required. Dexter’s should keep its written allergen information current and make it available to customers without charge.

Next stage: full menu allergen matrix

This page is the customer safety and legal framework. The next step is to link every live menu item and modifier to a verified allergen matrix based on the actual recipes, product labels and supplier information used by Dexter’s. Until an item has been verified, the website should not claim that it is “allergen free”.

Staff procedure

  • Never guess whether an allergen is present.
  • Check the current recipe/product label and the written allergen record.
  • Check sauces, toppings, sides and substitutions as well as the main item.
  • Explain any known cross-contact risk honestly.
  • If the information cannot be confirmed, tell the customer that rather than giving an unsafe answer.
