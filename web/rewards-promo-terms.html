Dexter’s rewards

Receipt QR Reward Promotion

Terms for the chance to win a free coffee or cake when an eligible Dexter’s Loyalty App receipt QR is scanned.

How to take part

  • Place an eligible collection order through the Dexter’s Loyalty App.
  • The order must be completed and marked Collected.
  • Sign in to the same customer account that placed the order and scan that order’s one-time receipt QR in the Loyalty App.
  • The first valid scan for that order enters the promotion. Repeated scans do not create additional entries.

No separate fee is charged for entry; entry is an incidental benefit of an eligible app order.

How the winner is selected

Eligible first-time receipt scans are entered into the promotion and winners are selected automatically by the Dexter’s promotion system.

If a receipt wins, the system assigns either Free Coffee or Free Cake. The result shown in the customer’s app is the promotion result for that receipt.

The prize

The prize is one free coffee or one free cake, as shown in the winner’s Dexter’s Loyalty App. The prize is personal to the winning customer account, has no cash alternative and cannot be claimed twice. Availability of individual products may vary; staff will confirm the available item when the reward is redeemed.

Points and the prize are separate

Collection-order points are normally added automatically when an app order is marked Collected. The receipt QR can recover missing points, but it cannot award the same order’s points twice. A receipt can still enter the coffee/cake promotion on its first valid scan even if the order’s points were already added automatically.

Eligibility and misuse

The promotion is for Dexter’s Loyalty App customer accounts and eligible app collection orders. Staff/admin accounts are not eligible through the customer claim route. Dexter’s may reject entries or rewards created by fraud, tampering, duplicated receipts, technical abuse or an obvious system error. Genuine entries already made should not be disadvantaged by later changes to the promotion.

Promotion period

The promotion starts on 3 September 2026 and is currently ongoing with no fixed closing date. If Dexter’s decides to end or materially change the promotion, the change should apply to future entries and be communicated through the Loyalty App or these terms as appropriate.

Promoter and questions

Promoter: Dexter’s, Glasgow, Scotland. Questions about the promotion can be sent to hello@dextersspot.co.uk or by calling 0141 473 5249.
