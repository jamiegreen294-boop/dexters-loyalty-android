Dexter’s rewards

Receipt QR Reward Promotion

Terms for the chance to receive a free coffee or cake when an eligible Dexter’s Loyalty App receipt QR is scanned.

Promoter

Promoter: Dexter’s, the food business operating the Dexter’s Loyalty App in Scotland. Contact hello@dextersspot.co.uk or 0141 473 5249. The promoter’s full legal trader name and postal/business address must be available in the app/legal information.

How to take part

  • Place an eligible collection order through the Dexter’s Loyalty App.
  • The order must be completed and marked Collected.
  • Sign in to the same customer account and scan that order’s one-time receipt QR.
  • The first valid scan for that eligible order enters/checks the promotion. Repeated scans do not create extra entries.

No separate entry fee is charged. The price of Dexter’s food is not increased because this promotion is running; customers pay the normal advertised price for their order whether or not the promotion is available.

Eligibility

The promotion is available to genuine Dexter’s Loyalty App customer accounts completing eligible app collection orders. Staff/admin test accounts, fraudulent accounts, duplicated receipts and technical-abuse attempts are not eligible through the customer claim route. Any age or geographic restriction introduced in future must be clearly stated before entry.

How results are determined

Eligible first-time receipt scans are processed automatically by the Dexter’s promotion system using the promotion mechanics configured for the relevant period. If a receipt is selected as a winner, the app assigns the prize shown. Dexter’s must administer the promotion fairly and must not alter a genuine result after entry except to correct fraud, duplicate use, tampering or an obvious technical error.

Prize

The prize is one free coffee or one free cake, as shown in the winning customer’s app. The prize is personal to the customer account, can be redeemed once, has no cash alternative and cannot be exchanged for cash. If the exact item is unavailable, Dexter’s may offer a reasonable equivalent of similar nature/value where appropriate.

Points are separate

Collection-order loyalty points and the receipt promotion are separate. A receipt QR may recover missing points where the system verifies they were not already credited, but it cannot award the same order’s points twice.

Promotion period and changes

The promotion started on 3 September 2026 and is currently ongoing without a fixed closing date. Dexter’s may close the promotion for future entries, but should communicate a closure or material change clearly in the app. Conditions affecting entries already validly made should be changed only where legally justified and fairly administered.

Misuse and technical faults

Dexter’s may reject or reverse entries/rewards created by fraud, tampering, duplicated receipts, manipulation, automated abuse or an obvious system error. A technical fault does not automatically create a right to a prize that was not validly awarded, but Dexter’s should investigate genuine customer reports fairly.

Consumer rights

Nothing in these promotion terms removes statutory rights relating to the underlying food order or any other rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
