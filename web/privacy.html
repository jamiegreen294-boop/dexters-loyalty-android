Customer information

Privacy & Data Protection

How Dexter’s handles personal information used by the website, Loyalty App, collection ordering and Sunday Roast pre-orders.

Who is responsible for your data?

Dexter’s is responsible for the personal information it collects through its website and Loyalty App. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@dextersspot.co.uk.

What we may collect

  • Name and contact details.
  • Account, loyalty, points, stamp and reward information.
  • Order details, collection times and order status.
  • Payment references and payment status. Full payment-card details are handled by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Dexter’s.
  • Technical information needed for security, sign-in and reliable operation.
  • Messages or enquiries you send to Dexter’s.

Why we use it

  • To create and manage accounts and loyalty benefits.
  • To accept, prepare and manage collection and Sunday Roast orders.
  • To confirm payments, prevent duplicate transactions and handle refunds where applicable.
  • To provide customer support and deal with complaints.
  • To protect the app, customers and business from fraud, misuse or security incidents.
  • To meet legal, tax, accounting and food-safety obligations where applicable.

Legal bases

Depending on the activity, Dexter’s may process information because it is necessary to perform a contract with you, because the business has a legitimate interest in operating and securing its services, because there is a legal obligation, or because you have given consent where consent is required.

Who information may be shared with

Information may be shared only where necessary with service providers used to run the website/app, authentication, database hosting, payment processing, communications and order fulfilment, and with authorities where required by law. Service providers should only receive the information needed for their role.

How long we keep information

Dexter’s should keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including legal, accounting, security and dispute-resolution requirements. Retention periods should be reviewed regularly rather than keeping customer information indefinitely.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, depending on the circumstances. You can also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact Dexter’s using the email above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Cookies and local storage

The website/app may use strictly necessary browser storage for sign-in, security and core features. If non-essential analytics, advertising or similar cookies are added, customers should be told clearly and asked for consent before those non-essential technologies are used.

Security

Dexter’s uses access controls and service-provider security features to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so access should be limited to authorised staff and credentials must not be shared.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the service or legal requirements change. The current version should always be available from the website and Loyalty App.
