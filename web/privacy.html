Customer information

Privacy & Data Protection

How Dexter’s handles personal information used by the website, Loyalty App, collection ordering, rewards and Sunday Roast pre-orders.

Who is responsible for your data?

Dexter’s is the controller of personal information collected through its customer website and Loyalty App. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@dextersspot.co.uk or call 0141 473 5249.

What we collect

  • Name, email address, phone number and account details.
  • Loyalty stamps, points, rewards, offers and redemption history.
  • Order contents, modifiers, notes, collection times and order status.
  • Sunday Roast booking, deposit reference and payment status. Full card details are processed by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Dexter’s.
  • Security, authentication and technical information needed to operate the service.
  • Messages, enquiries, complaints and catering information you send to Dexter’s.

Why we use it and our lawful bases

  • Contract: to create accounts, process orders, take deposits, provide loyalty benefits and respond to requests connected with an order or service.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, improve and secure Dexter’s services, prevent fraud and duplicate rewards, maintain business records and provide customer support where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where records are needed for tax, accounting, food-safety or other legal requirements.
  • Consent: where the law requires consent, for example for non-essential cookies or particular marketing communications.

Who we use to provide the service

Dexter’s uses service providers for hosting and delivery of the website/app, database and authentication, payment processing, email and communications. Current services include Vercel, Supabase, Stripe and Google/Gmail, and WhatsApp/Meta where you choose to contact Dexter’s through WhatsApp. These providers process only the information needed for their service and operate under their own security and data-protection obligations.

International processing

Some technology providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, Dexter’s relies on the safeguards or transfer mechanisms made available by the relevant provider and applicable law.

How long we keep information

Dexter’s does not intend to keep personal information indefinitely. Account and loyalty information is kept while the account is active and as needed to complete deletion, security and dispute handling. Order, payment-reference and business records are kept for the period reasonably required for customer service, accounting, tax and legal obligations. Support and security records are kept only as long as they remain reasonably necessary for the issue or security purpose. These periods are reviewed when systems or legal requirements change.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent. To exercise a right, contact Dexter’s using the details above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ICO data-protection complaints.

Cookies and local storage

The website/app uses browser storage needed for sign-in, security and core functionality. Non-essential analytics, advertising or similar technologies should not be activated without the notice and consent required by law.

Security

Dexter’s uses authentication, access controls, one-time reward checks and service-provider security features. Staff access should be limited to authorised users and credentials must not be shared. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This notice may be updated when Dexter’s changes its services, providers or legal requirements. The current version is made available through the website and Loyalty App.

Last reviewed: 3 September 2026. This notice is intended to describe Dexter’s current customer-data practices; it does not replace tailored legal advice.
