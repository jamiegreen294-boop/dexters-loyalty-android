Sunday Roast Deposit & Refund Policy

Deposit

A £4.99 deposit is required for each adult or kids Sunday Roast meal ordered. The deposit is part-payment of the meal price, not an additional charge. Extras do not create another £4.99 deposit unless clearly stated before ordering.

Remaining balance

The deposit is deducted from the full order total. The remaining balance is due on collection. Adult roast £14.99 = £4.99 deposit + £10.00 balance. Kids roast £9.99 = £4.99 deposit + £5.00 balance. Multiple meals and extras are calculated as the full order total less deposits already paid.

Customer cancellation

Sunday Roasts are fresh food prepared for a specific collection date/time and may fall within exceptions to ordinary distance-selling cancellation rights for rapidly perishable goods. If you need to cancel, contact Dexter’s as soon as possible. Where preparation has not begun and Dexter’s can reasonably release the reserved food/slot without loss, Dexter’s may refund the deposit. Once ingredients have been specifically committed, preparation has begun or the agreed collection time is imminent, the deposit may be retained to reflect the reserved food and preparation commitment, subject always to statutory rights.

Failure to collect

If a customer fails to collect at the agreed time without prior agreement, the deposit will normally be retained because food, preparation time and a collection slot were reserved for that order. Any amount retained must not remove a remedy the customer is legally entitled to.

When Dexter’s cancels or cannot fulfil

If Dexter’s cancels the order or cannot provide the ordered meal, the applicable deposit and any other amount paid for the unavailable order will be refunded through the appropriate payment route unless the customer voluntarily accepts an agreed alternative.

Problems with the food or service

Nothing in this policy limits rights where food is unsafe, faulty, not as described, or where a service is not supplied with reasonable care and skill. Contact Dexter’s promptly so the issue can be investigated and an appropriate remedy provided where due.

Failed or incomplete payment

A Sunday Roast order is not confirmed until the required deposit is successfully processed and the ordering flow confirms acceptance. If payment fails, is cancelled or expires, no confirmed pre-order is created unless Dexter’s separately confirms otherwise.

Collection and loyalty points

The outstanding balance must be paid before the order is treated as fully paid/collected. Eligible loyalty points are awarded under the current Loyalty Programme rules once the qualifying order is completed.

Allergens

Review Allergen Information before placing the pre-order. Allergen information must be available before purchase is concluded and again when the food is supplied.
