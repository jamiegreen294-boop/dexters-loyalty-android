Sunday Roast Deposit & Refund Policy

£4.99 deposit per meal

A £4.99 deposit is required for each adult or kids Sunday Roast meal ordered.

  • 1 meal = £4.99 deposit
  • 2 meals = £9.98 deposit
  • 3 meals = £14.97 deposit
  • 4 meals = £19.96 deposit

Extras do not create an additional £4.99 deposit.

Remaining balance

The deposit is deducted from the full order total. The entire remaining balance must be paid on the day of collection.

  • Adult roast £14.99 → £4.99 deposit + £10.00 due on collection
  • Kids roast £9.99 → £4.99 deposit + £5.00 due on collection

For orders with multiple meals or extras, the balance due is the full order total minus the total deposit already paid.

Non-collection

If a customer fails to collect at the agreed Sunday collection time, the deposit already paid for that order will normally be retained because food, preparation time and a collection slot were reserved specifically for that pre-order.

When Dexter's refunds the deposit

If Dexter's cancels the order or cannot fulfil it, the applicable deposit will be refunded.

Nothing in this policy removes statutory rights that may apply under UK consumer law.

Failed or incomplete Stripe payment

A Sunday Roast order is not confirmed until Stripe successfully processes the required deposit.

If the payment fails, is cancelled or expires, the order is not confirmed and is not sent to Dexter's Sunday Roast.

Collection and loyalty points

Staff must confirm the outstanding balance has been paid in full before the order is marked collected.

Eligible loyalty points are awarded once the full Sunday Roast order has been paid, under the loyalty programme's current rules.

Contact

For questions about a Sunday Roast pre-order, deposit or refund, contact Dexter's using the support details shown on the Dexter's website or Stripe checkout page.
