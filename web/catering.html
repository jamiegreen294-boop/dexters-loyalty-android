Tell us what you need

We’ll ask for:

  • event or collection date and preferred time
  • number of people
  • exact food items and quantities
  • drinks and extras
  • vegetarian or other dietary requirements
  • allergies and intolerances
  • budget, if relevant
  • name, phone number and email
  • packaging or special instructions

We confirm the order

Your catering request is not confirmed until Dexter’s has checked availability, agreed the details with you and confirmed the order. We may ask for payment or a deposit depending on the size and nature of the order.

Allergies & intolerances

If anyone in your group has a food allergy or intolerance, tell us before the order is confirmed. Do not rely on menu names alone. Ingredients, recipes and supplier products can change.

We will provide the allergen information available for the specific items being ordered. Because food is prepared in a working kitchen, we cannot promise an allergen-free environment unless we have specifically confirmed that in writing for the item concerned.
