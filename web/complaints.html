Complaints Policy

Contact Dexter’s

If something has gone wrong with an order, payment, reward, account or service, contact Dexter’s as soon as reasonably possible.

Information that helps us investigate

Where relevant, provide your name, order/reference number, date/time, the issue, photographs or other evidence and the outcome you are seeking. Do not send full payment-card details or passwords.

How complaints are handled

Dexter’s should acknowledge and investigate complaints fairly, review relevant order and system records, and provide an appropriate response or remedy where one is due. Food-safety or allergen complaints should be treated as urgent and escalated internally.

Privacy complaints

For concerns about personal information, use the same contact details. Customers also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Consumer rights

Contacting Dexter’s first does not remove or delay any statutory right or remedy. Customers may use available court, regulatory, card/payment-provider or consumer remedies where applicable.

Scotland

Dexter’s operates in Scotland. These complaint arrangements do not prevent a customer from seeking independent advice, including from Citizens Advice Scotland or an appropriate regulator where relevant.
