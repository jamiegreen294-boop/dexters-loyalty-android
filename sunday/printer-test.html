Sunday customer printer test

Print one customer’s Sunday order to the Epson. Your phone or tablet must be on the same Wi-Fi as the printer, including when testing at home. Nothing on this page accepts an order or changes its status.

1. Choose the ticket

For real orders, sign in to the loyalty app as staff or admin on this same website first. The sample needs no sign-in.

2. Check and print

Enter the printer’s current address. It can change when moving from shop Wi-Fi to home Wi-Fi. This test uses HTTPS and does not change the KDS printer settings.





After sending, check the paper at the printer. If confirmation is missing, check for a ticket before retrying to avoid duplicates.
