Dexter's Buffet Menu

Breakfast Rolls — £4.25pp

One hot roll per person. Choose a mix of Square Sausage, Bacon, Links x2, Potato Scone x2, Fried Egg or Scrambled Egg. Brown and tomato sauce supplied.

Lunch Platter — £6.95pp

Mixed Dexter's cold rolls and wraps, cut for sharing. Fillings can include Ham Salad, Turkey Salad, Chopped Pork & Ham, Cheese & Ham, Cheese & Tomato, Cheese & Onion, Chicken Tikka and Tandoori Chicken.

Classic Glasgow Buffet — £9.95pp

Mixed sandwiches & wraps, mini sausage rolls, mini pizza slices, Dexter's Chicken Tenders, vegetable pakora/samosas, mini spring rolls, cocktail sausages, Walkers Ready Salted Crisps and Dexter's dips.

Street Feast — £12.95pp

Dexter's Street Sub pieces, pizza slices, Chicken Tenders, Garlic Bread, Onion Rings, mini sausage rolls, pakora/samosas and dips. Sub choices can include Chicken Mayo Melt, Southern Fried Chicken, Chicken Tikka, Pepperoni & Cheese, Philly Steak and Chicken & Bacon.

Hot Chinese Buffet — £12.95pp

Choose two mains from Chicken Breast Curry, Chicken Szechuan, Black Bean Chicken, Garlic Chicken, Sweet & Sour Chicken or Salt & Chilli Chicken. Served with Boiled Rice, Chips, mini spring rolls and Prawn Crackers.

Buffet Extras

Mini quiche · sausage rolls · cocktail sausages · mini pizzas · vegetable pakora/samosas · mini spring rolls · chicken strips · fruit platter · cakes/traybakes · Walkers Ready Salted Crisps · salad/coleslaw · dips.
